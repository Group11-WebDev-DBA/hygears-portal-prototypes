About & Contact

A student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila.

What Hygears Does

In the organization’s own words: “The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle…”

Members build energy-efficient electric vehicles, the Luna series, and compete in the Shell Eco-marathon, an energy-efficiency competition rather than a race.

Borrowing Rules

  1. Reserve through the portal first. Officers approve or decline each request.
  2. Keep your reference number. Use it on Track Request.
  3. Bring your PUP ID when you pick up.
  4. Return tools by the return date on your request. Consumables are used up and not returned.
  5. Longest borrowing period: from officers
  6. Late or damaged items: policy from officers
