About and contact

Who we are

PUP Hygears is a student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila.

“The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle...”

The team builds the Luna series of energy-efficient electric vehicles and competes in the Shell Eco-marathon, a competition about how far a car goes on the least energy, not how fast.

Borrowing rules

Draft, officers to confirm
  1. Reserve online first and keep your reference number.
  2. Wait for Approved on Track Request before you go to pick up.
  3. Bring your PUP ID when you pick up.
  4. Return tools on or before your return date, clean and complete.
  5. Report damage or a missing part right away.
  6. Consumables are used up, so they are not returned.
