About Hygears

Engineering Innovation • Research • Technology • Leadership

Who we are

PUP Hygears is a student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila. In our own words, it "aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle."

The team builds the Luna series of energy-efficient electric vehicles and competes in the Shell Eco-marathon, a competition for the most energy-efficient car, not the fastest.

Members come from

  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electronics and Communications Engineering
  • Industrial Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Computer Engineering
  • Marketing

Borrowing rules

Draft, officers to confirm
  1. Reserve on this site first. Walk-in borrowing depends on what's left on the wall.
  2. Bring your PUP ID when you pick up. The officer on duty checks it against your request.
  3. Return tools on or before your return date, in the condition you got them.
  4. Report damage or missing parts when you return the tool, not later.
  5. Consumables are for project use. Take only what your request says.
