# PUP Hygears brand notes (from their Facebook page, Nov 22, 2026)

Source: facebook.com/PUPHygears (posts, photos, albums, profile-picture history). Use this to keep the portal on brand.

## What stays the same
- **The mark:** the "HG" logo, speed lines on the H and a gear for the G. It has been the same shape across every profile picture since the old days. Only its color and texture change per campaign.
- **Classic colors:** red and white (and white on black). Older profile pictures are a red square with a white mark, or a white mark on black. The current year's look is a warmer orange-red.
- **Sign-off on every post:**
  ```
  ——
  PUP Hygears
  Engineering Innovation • Research • Technology • Leadership
  #GEARINGUpTowardExcellence
  ```
  "Engineering Innovation • Research • Technology • Leadership" is their standing descriptor. It works as the site's tagline or footer line.
- **Hashtags they reuse:** #GEARINGUpTowardExcellence (main), #PUPHygears2627 (this school year), #TheAdventureREContinues.
- **Credits:** posts credit the caption writer and the pubmat (graphic) maker by name.

## What changes per campaign
Each event or recruitment drive gets its own visual skin, built around the same mark:
- **PHYNIX 2026 Research Workshop** (the current cover): dark background, fire, gold and phoenix wings, ornate serif display type.
- **ASCEND recruitment:** cream background, orange-red puzzle pieces, pixel or block display type. "Find your missing piece / Be part of the puzzle."
- **General Assembly photobooth:** deep purple-blue with a white starburst, white logo.
- **Other profile versions:** yellow, black and white, red with a network graphic, an ID-badge design.

So the site should NOT copy one campaign's art. It should use the stable identity (mark, red, white, warm neutrals) as a calm frame that campaign graphics can sit inside, for example announcement cards that show the current pubmat.

## Tone of voice
- **Language:** mostly Tagalog for community and hype posts, English for formal and announcement posts, often mixed.
- **Style:** energetic, warm and proud. Headlines in Unicode bold or italic, lots of emoji in recruitment posts (🧩✨🧡), dramatic poetic lines for big events ("Mula sa datos patungong kilos").
- **Words they use:** "keep the gears turning", "gearing up", "missing piece", members called "Merakis" (this year), "Nex-thinkers, N-explorers, Nex-creators" (PHYNIX).
- **For the website:** keep the warmth and pride but stay clear and readable. Plain bold headings instead of Unicode fonts, at most light emoji, and the standing descriptor line. Short Tagalog touches are fine for the community side.

## Suggested site palette
- **Primary:** red, about `#D7261E`. It's the classic logo red and passes contrast as a button with white text.
- **Warm accent:** orange or amber, used sparingly for highlights only, never for small text on cream.
- **Neutrals:** warm cream or white backgrounds and deep brown-black text, with the white-on-black logo version for the dark footer.
