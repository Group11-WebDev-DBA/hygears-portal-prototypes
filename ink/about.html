About Hygears

A student research organization at PUP Sta. Mesa building efficient electric vehicles.

The Luna series

Hygears designs and builds the Luna cars and takes them to the Shell Eco-marathon, a competition in energy efficiency rather than speed.

“We don’t just build vehicles, we build people to help us build the future!”

Borrowing rules

Draft rules. The officers confirm the final list.

  1. Reserve online first. Pickups are for approved requests only.
  2. Bring your PUP ID when you pick an item up.
  3. Return tools by the return date on your request, in the state you got them.
  4. Consumables are used up and need no return date.
  5. Tell an officer right away if a tool breaks or goes missing.
