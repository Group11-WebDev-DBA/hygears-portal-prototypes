About PUP Hygears

A student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila.

Who we are

"The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle..."

We build the Luna series of energy-efficient electric vehicles and compete in the Shell Eco-marathon, an energy-efficiency competition, not a race. The org's albums go back to the Shell Eco-marathon 2014 and 2015.

"We don't just build vehicles, we build people to help us build the future!"

Borrowing rules

Draft. These follow how the portal works. The officers will confirm the final rules.

  1. 1

    Reserve first

    Send a reservation through this portal before picking anything up.

  2. 2

    Wait for approval

    Only approved requests can be picked up. Check on Track Request.

  3. 3

    Return tools on time

    Return tools on or before your return date. Consumables are used up and not returned.

  4. 4

    Report damage

    Tell an officer right away if a tool breaks or goes missing.
