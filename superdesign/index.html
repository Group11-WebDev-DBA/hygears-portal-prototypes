    How borrowing works

    1. 1

      Reserve

      Pick an item, choose your pickup date and send the form. You get a reference number.

    2. 2

      Wait for approval

      An officer approves or declines it. Check anytime on Track Request.

    3. 3

      Pick up and return

      Collect it at the workshop. Return tools by the return date; consumables are yours to use up.
