About Hygears

Engineering Innovation • Research • Technology • Leadership

Who we are

"The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle."

We build energy-efficient electric vehicles, the Luna series, and compete in the Shell Eco-marathon. It is an energy-efficiency competition: the goal is to go the farthest on the least energy, not to finish first.

Borrowing rules

Draft, to confirm with officers

  1. Reserve online first. Only approved requests can be picked up.

  2. Bring your reference number when you pick up. ID requirement

  3. Return tools on or before the return date, clean and complete.

  4. Consumables are used up and are not returned. Take only what your project needs.

  5. Report damaged or lost items to an officer right away. replacement policy
