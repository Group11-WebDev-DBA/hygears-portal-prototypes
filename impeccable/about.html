About Hygears

A student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila.

What we do

In our own words: "The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle."

We design and build energy-efficient electric vehicles, the Luna series, and compete in the Shell Eco-marathon, a competition that rewards energy efficiency rather than speed. Hygears teams took part in the Shell Eco-marathon in 2014 and 2015.

Who joins

Members come from Mechanical, Electronics and Communications, Industrial, Electrical and Computer Engineering, plus Marketing. Work is split across five committees:

  • Finance and Operations
  • Internal Affairs
  • Innovation and Research
  • External Affairs
  • Creative Media and Design

Borrowing rules

Draft, to confirm with officers

  1. Reserve online first and wait for approval. Track it with your reference number.
  2. Bring your PUP ID when you pick up an item.
  3. Return tools on or before the return date, clean and with all parts.
  4. Consumables are used up and not returned. Take only what your project needs.
  5. Report damage or loss to an officer right away.
