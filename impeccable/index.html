    Who we are

    A student research organization at PUP Sta. Mesa. Members from Mechanical, Electronics and Communications, Industrial, Electrical and Computer Engineering, and Marketing design, build and race-prepare energy-efficient electric vehicles. The Shell Eco-marathon scores how far a car goes on the least energy, not who finishes first.
