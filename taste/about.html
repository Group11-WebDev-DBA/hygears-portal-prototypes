About PUP Hygears

A student research organization at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, that builds energy-efficient electric vehicles.

The org and the Luna series

“The PUP Hygears is an organization that aims to further enhance the awareness of the PUP Students in the field of research and help produce well-trained and competent students through real-life training in innovation and production of a highly efficient vehicle…”

The team builds the Luna series and competes in the Shell Eco-marathon, an energy-efficiency competition: cars score on how far they go on the least energy, not on speed.

Programs members come from

  • Electronics and Communications Engineering
  • Industrial Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering

Borrowing rules

Draft

Draft wording for the officers to confirm or replace.

  1. Who can borrow

    PUP students with a valid ID, for coursework and research projects.

  2. Reserve first

    Send the reservation form and wait for the approval email before pickup.

  3. Return tools on time

    Tools come back on the return date, clean and complete. Late returns pause your next reservation.

  4. Consumables are used up

    Filament, tubing, zip ties, tape and epoxy are not returned. Take only what the project needs.

  5. Report damage

    Tell an officer at return if something broke. It helps the next borrower.
